About DOREN Advisory

Why DOREN Advisory exists, how it works, and who's behind it.

Heritage

Why this, why now.

DOREN Advisory started from a simple observation: most early-stage companies don't fail from a lack of effort — they fail from a lack of clarity. Founders are moving fast, wearing every hat, and rarely have the bandwidth to step back and get their positioning right.

The firm works hands-on, embedded alongside founders and operators rather than at a distance — sharpening how the brand shows up, building the community around it, and training teams to carry that momentum forward long after the engagement ends.

Current work centers on market positioning and community-building for early-stage companies, including a partnership with AGMNT, a fashion-tech startup, to sharpen their positioning and grow the community around their events.

Founder

Logan Del Rosario

Founder & Consultant, DOREN Advisory

Logan is a Business Economics student at the University of California, Riverside and a Regents Scholar, with a background in market analysis, brand positioning, and audience growth.

Alongside DOREN Advisory, Logan built and monetized a 100,000+ person digital audience (50M+ views) across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube — giving the firm a practical, data-driven lens on positioning, audience-building, and community growth.
